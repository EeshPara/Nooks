# Nooks recorded ambience

The three original rain/fire recordings below are CC0 1.0. Verified 2026-10-08. No new subscriptions or credits used.

- `rain-window.mp3`: “Rain sound effect - Gentle rain on window” by Legnalegna55, https://freesound.org/people/Legnalegna55/sounds/553390/ . Public high-quality preview https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/553/553390_10485775-hq.mp3 . Used for Rainy Library.
- `stove-fire.mp3` and `hearth-fire.mp3`: “Fire crackling in fireplace” by Davor, https://freesound.org/people/Davor/sounds/382616/ . Public high-quality preview https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/382/382616_7094269-hq.mp3 . Separate excerpts for Howl’s stove and Gryffindor’s hearth.
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Edits: 36-second excerpts; high-pass at 80 Hz; low-pass at 6200 Hz for window rain, 1600 Hz for the enclosed stove, 4200 Hz for the open hearth. Gentle transient compression, -20 LUFS target, -5 dBTP ceiling. Two-second edge crossfade produces 34-second loops. Stereo, 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps MP3. Spotify remains a separate playback source.
