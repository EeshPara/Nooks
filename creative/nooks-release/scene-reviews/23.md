Verdict: **needs_revision**

Seam/typical: 1.330; outside-mask change: 0.000166.

Required: Restrict ocean motion to open water, excluding the complete sailboat and its immediate silhouette as well as all cliff/rock geometry with a safe feather margin. Preserve the original geometry; independently review the new exact hash.
