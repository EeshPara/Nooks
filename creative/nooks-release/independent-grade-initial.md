# Independent initial quality and readiness grade

Reviewed 2026-10-08 by the independent grader. This is an interim assessment while implementation continues, not release approval. No deployment, database mutation, or feature implementation was performed by this grader.

## Verdict

**Product quality: 6/10, provisional. Public multi-user production readiness: 2/10, not established.** These are evidence-weighted scores, not percentages of completed code. Strong artwork and careful playback foundations coexist with incomplete animation coverage, unverified listening quality, and an unconfigured public account deployment. Passing local tests cannot establish public capacity or real-account correctness.

The parent reports 292 native/backend and 61 web tests passing, audio regression checks passing, and 11 playback tests passing. Those runs were not independently repeated here. The grader independently read current source, asset manifests, technical media reports, project instructions, and release acceptance records, and visually inspected the Alpine Cabin and Neon Tokyo trial contact sheets plus mask overview sheets 1 and 8. This review does not claim continuous video playback, headphone audition, or a live user journey.

## Quality observations

- The artwork is visually coherent, detailed, warm and convincingly spatial. In inspected mask sheets, foreground desks, books and framing are generally protected while motion regions target windows, fire and water. These are good constraints, not proof of final output quality.
- Alpine Cabin's reviewed contact frames preserve the surrounding desk and mountain scene while fire changes inside the hearth. Technical metrics pass: seam ratio 1.237, outside-mask mean change 0.00110, no audio tracks. It is a promising trial; continuous playback still needs review.
- Neon Tokyo's contact frames retain stable furniture and pleasant lighting. Its loop metric failure is a concrete reason to hold it rather than calling it approved.
- The scene manifest matches artwork paths before allowing motion, with custom/alternate scene guards. Playback source handles reduced motion, visibility, failed play, disposal and stale play cancellation. These are strong implementation choices.
- Fifty explicit natural ambience presets and scene beds exist, with synthetic channels off by default. Licensing/edit records distinguish the CC BY stream recording and expose attribution. Ten reusable recordings across fifty mixes provide coverage, not fifty independently captured acoustic environments.

## Exact acceptance gates for final grading

1. **Catalog:** 50 unique catalog IDs, 50 source-matched approved motion entries, all deployed URLs return decodable assets; manifest references have no missing or duplicate room IDs. Record hashes and bytes of final reviewed output.
2. **Animation quality:** every final clip fully decodes, has no audio, passes current technical gates, and is visually reviewed in motion for at least 30 seconds. Stable architecture/objects, plausible local movement, no camera drift or visible loop reset. Crossfade seams require particular attention. A contact sheet alone is insufficient.
3. **Audio quality:** no automatic sound before user gesture; all ten sources and all distinct scene mixes auditioned through three seams; volume, mute, pause, rapid scene switches, tab visibility, suspended AudioContext and decoding/network failure behave predictably. No false listening claims.
4. **Asset performance:** audio fetched only on demand; opening the picker does not fetch all fifty videos. Test cold and cached navigation on desktop and a constrained mobile profile, confirm useful first interaction, and record initial bytes and request counts. Native allowed origins and actual host playback must be checked after externalizing audio.
5. **Two real accounts:** sign in, refresh, expire, sign out and switch accounts on the public origin. Create/join/leave a nook; verify invitation expiry/revocation, owner removal/archive, live presence, foreign/private read/write denial, and persistence after reopening. Record no secret or study content in evidence.
6. **Realtime correctness:** a change from user A appears for B without manual reload; disconnect/reconnect converges to authoritative state; duplicate/out-of-order notifications do not corrupt state; removal promptly removes access. Record observed update delay and fallback behavior.
7. **Capacity:** define 1,000 concurrently connected users, room distribution, heartbeat frequency and actual read/write mix. Suggested acceptance budget, subject to capacity planning: 30-minute steady state, API p95 below 1 second and p99 below 3 seconds, live update p95 below 2 seconds, unexpected HTTP errors below 1%, zero authorization/integrity errors. Include bounded bursts and recovery, provider limits and headroom. Do not equate synthetic accounts, seeded rows or mocked sockets with deployed users.
8. **Recovery and operations:** actual native unsaved-change recovery; hosted restore covering SQL, identities and private artwork bytes into an isolated destination; measured RPO/RTO; a named operator and verified privacy-safe alert delivery. Preserve production data.
9. **Release identity:** selected changes ported deliberately across the three distinct source snapshots, exact built source and deployment IDs recorded, post-deploy public/browser and actual native smoke checks completed. A build success or private health endpoint alone is not a launch sign-off.

Regrade after fixes and fresh evidence. Do not report this preliminary score as a comprehensive UX review or proof of safety at scale.
