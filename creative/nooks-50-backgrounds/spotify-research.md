# Spotify recommendations for the 50 Nooks

Researched 2026-10-07. `spotify-research.json` contains all 50 IDs from `scenes.json` exactly once, with 34 distinct public Spotify playlist URLs. No runtime files were edited. The titles and curators are copied from accessible Spotify page or search metadata; IDs were taken from observed URLs, never generated. Each record includes the supporting source URL and a verification note.

## Selection approach

Specific fandom selections cover Stardew Valley, Animal Crossing, Pokémon, Zelda, Minecraft, Harry Potter, Hufflepuff, Middle-earth, ACOTAR, Fourth Wing, Frieren, Sumeru, One Piece, and Studio Ghibli. They are independently curated recommendations, not playlists owned by Nooks. Most fandom choices are fan or label playlists; their inclusion does not establish endorsement by a rights holder.

Slytherin uses gothic classical music, Ravenclaw uses Hogwarts library ambience, and the two Sanrio rooms use kawaii lo-fi. Coraline and Wednesday share an instrumental gothic/classical selection with film music. Original rooms use setting-appropriate reading lo-fi, piano, jazz, acoustic guitar, fantasy, or ambient music. Repeated choices are intentional where the settings share a listening mood. Descriptions are editorial recommendations rather than guarantees about every track.

The Seoul café selection includes vocals. The tea-time, fantasy-book, and One Piece playlists were selected using visible metadata rather than a complete audition. The Sumeru selection is regional, not specifically the Akademiya soundtrack. Ghibli choices span multiple films rather than claiming to be complete individual-film soundtracks.

## Verification limits

Search-accessible metadata does **not** establish playback availability. No authenticated playback, regional licensing, Spotify account behavior, full-track access, or embed playback was tested. Pages were accessed through web search/open tools, which can return cached snapshots; many direct pages exposed only title metadata. Playlist content, title, curator, privacy, and availability can change. Some URLs work in search metadata even when a direct tool fetch returns a cache miss, including the autumn playlist. A cache miss is not proof that a playlist is removed.

The links use the public `open.spotify.com/playlist/{id}` form, with localized or embed source URLs retained when those supplied the evidence. No OAuth, developer-app registration, private account data, or paid service was used. This research supports outbound links and recommendations; it is not a promise that every playlist can play inside Nooks without Spotify sign-in.
