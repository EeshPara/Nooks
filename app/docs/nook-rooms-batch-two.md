# Nook room artwork — batch two

Eight original project-bound backgrounds, generated with the built-in image tool. All original PNGs and runtime WebPs are 1672 × 941 pixels. Original PNGs are archived unchanged in `assets/originals/images`. Every image was visually inspected for composition, style, and unwanted characters or interface text. Final files live in `ui/public/images/lofi-<id>.webp`; the structured metadata is in `docs/nook-rooms-batch-two.json`.

The first four use cinematic painted art. The other four were regenerated after the request for varied visual styles, replacing their initial cinematic versions. No companions or animation behavior are enabled by these assets.

## Research and interpretation

These are original imagined study spaces, not reproductions of source photography. Primary-source research informed or checked environmental motifs. Fantasy interiors do not claim engineering or historical accuracy.

- **Desert:** [National Park Service earthen architecture FAQs](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/earthen-architecture-faqs.htm) and [Tumacácori adobe bricks](https://www.nps.gov/tuma/learn/historyculture/adobe-bricks.htm) provide context for earthen construction. The sunset casita is an artistic interpretation.
- **Ocean:** [Monterey Bay Aquarium's kelp forest](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/exhibits/kelp-forest) supports the kelp-and-filtered-light environmental motif. The enclosed study is fictional.
- **Moon:** [NASA lunar surface image gallery](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/image-galleries/views-from-the-lunar-surface/) provides lunar landscape and Earth-view references; [NASA Moonbase visualization](https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase-environment-visualization/) gives habitat concept context. The warm study interior is imaginative.
- **Amsterdam:** [I amsterdam's architectural style guide](https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/see-and-do/attractions-and-sights/amsterdams-architectural-style) provides context for canal houses and gabled facades. The window viewpoint and room are invented.
- **Airship, treehouse, cottage, campus:** original thematic interpretations. Labels describe the mood and visual medium, rather than claiming a specific historical vehicle, address, university, or viewpoint.
