# Nook soundtracks

Verified 2026-10-03 against public Spotify playlist pages and Spotify's embed pages. This is a selection of existing playlists matched to Nooks settings. These playlists remain owned and maintained by their listed curators; Nooks has not created new Spotify playlists or copied their tracks. Track lists, names, availability, and Spotify playback limits can change.

The catalogue contains 14 distinct selections for the 31 built-in nooks and two unlockable annexes. The setting-specific descriptions below are Nooks editorial copy. Playlist names and curator credits retain their source spelling. Unknown or custom nooks receive Lofi Girl's study playlist until a user supplies another link.

## Maintaining the catalogue

`ui/src/world/nookPlaylists.ts` is the sole map from nook ID to the suggested soundtrack. `getNookPlaylist` also covers custom IDs. A selection is a suggestion, not an autoplay command: switching nooks should never replace a playlist the user is already playing without their choice. Keep user-supplied Spotify links available.

When changing a selection, open its Spotify page, verify the title and credited curator, check that it is public and fits the nook, update this record, and run `ui/src/world/nookPlaylists.test.mjs`. The tests check built-in/annex coverage, concise metadata, safe canonical links accepted by the real player, consistent curator credit, musical variety, and custom-ID fallback. They do not claim the external playlist will remain available forever.
